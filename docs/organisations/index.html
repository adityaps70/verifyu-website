How it works for an institution

Three moving parts.
Nothing exotic.

Register people, agree who may use VerifyU on your behalf, and rehearse what happens in an emergency.

  1. Step 01

    Registration drive

    Your people register themselves, free, with your support — their own details, contacts and the medical information they choose.

  2. Step 02

    Permitted access

    The responders, staff or volunteers you nominate use VerifyU within the arrangement agreed with your organisation. Owner verification

  3. Step 03

    Emergency workflow

    When a registered person cannot communicate, an authorised user looks for a match and reaches next of kin through the permitted workflow.

VerifyU supports identification and communication. It does not replace emergency services, and it can only help where the person is registered and a match is returned.

Where it fits

Six settings.
One registered identity.

Each setting has a different problem and a different workflow. The identity — and the person’s control over it — stays the same.

01 / 06

Hospitals

The problemAn unidentified or unconscious arrival — no documents, no phone, nobody to ask.

  1. Authorised user looks for a match
  2. Emergency information on screen
  3. Next of kin called through the app

What becomes possibleFamily reached sooner, with a record of who was contacted.

02 / 06

Government

The problemPublic emergencies where a person cannot be identified and family cannot be traced quickly.

  1. District registration drive
  2. Defined access for nominated responder groups
  3. Reporting scoped at the start

What becomes possibleA consented identity layer responders can check before someone is treated as unknown. Owner verification

Discuss a district pilot
03 / 06

Schools & colleges

The problemStaff need guardian contacts and medical information for a student who cannot answer for themselves.

  1. Registration during orientation
  2. Guardians listed as emergency contacts
  3. Student volunteers help others register

What becomes possibleIdentity, guardian contacts and blood group travel with the student — to the ground, the hostel, the field trip. Owner verification

Run a campus safety pilot
04 / 06

Residential & senior communities

The problemA resident is found unwell or disoriented and the person helping does not know who to call.

  1. Society registration drive
  2. Assisted registration for senior residents
  3. Awareness for residents and the security desk

What becomes possibleNeighbours, security staff and family connected through one registered profile instead of a phone tree. Owner verification

Bring VerifyU to your community
05 / 06

Events

The problemParticipants separated from their group, or unwell, with nothing on them but a bib number.

  1. Registration opened before the event
  2. Briefed volunteers and medical desk
  3. A named contact for the day

What becomes possibleA crowd in which a registered participant can be connected back to family sooner. Owner verification

Bring VerifyU to your event
06 / 06

Workplaces

The problemAn employee has an emergency away from the office and the site team has no reliable contact information.

  1. Registration offered as a safety benefit
  2. First-aid and security teams as VerifyU users
  3. Travel-safety briefings

What becomes possibleEmergency contacts that travel with the employee rather than with the HR file. Owner verification
