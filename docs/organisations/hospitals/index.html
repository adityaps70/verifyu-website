Hospitals

The problem

The first hour is spent on a question, not a treatment.

The clinical work starts immediately; everything that depends on knowing who the patient is has to wait.

  1. 01

    Arrivals without a name

    A road-accident casualty, an unconscious patient, an older person who walked away from home. Care begins; identity does not arrive with them.

  2. 02

    Family reached late

    Consent, admission and belongings all wait on one question the patient cannot answer: who should we call?

  3. 03

    Unknown clinical history

    Blood group, allergies and medication have to be established from scratch while the clock runs.

We do not publish statistics on unidentified arrivals. These are the situations hospital teams describe to us; your own department data is the only measure worth planning against.

The workflow

Five steps, inside the workflow you already run.

Step 01

Emergency arrival

A patient who cannot speak, or cannot be understood, arrives. Clinical care begins as it always does — VerifyU changes nothing about treatment.

Step 02

Identity support

An authorised VerifyU user on your team opens the scanner and looks for a match against registered profiles. A match depends on prior registration, a suitable image and connectivity.

Step 03

Useful emergency information

A returned profile opens marked “Scanned securely via VerifyU” — photo, name, gender and blood group on the header, with Personal, Emergency and Medical tabs behind it.

Step 04

Next of kin

The Emergency tab lists the two contacts the patient chose, with relation, number and a call button. Medical details open only after an OTP from one of those contacts.

Step 05

Response coordination

The department records who was identified, who was contacted and when — the handover discipline you already run, with one fewer unknown in it.

Straight away

On the verified profile

  • Photo and name — “Scanned securely via VerifyU”
  • Gender and blood group on the header
  • Personal details the patient recorded
  • Two emergency contacts with call buttons
Only with permission

After next-of-kin OTP

  • Medical details — only after an OTP from one of the patient’s own contacts
  • Nothing the person did not enter — VerifyU is not a clinical record
  • No bulk access — a lookup for the person in front of you, not a directory
  • No diagnosis — it supports your clinicians, never substitutes for them

Governance

Access your board can explain.

Facial data and emergency information are sensitive. The arrangement is built around consent, defined access and a record of what happened.

01

Consent-based registration

Nobody is added by the hospital. Each person registers themselves and chooses what the profile carries.

02

Emergency-relevant first

A match returns the profile header and emergency contacts. Medical details stay behind an OTP.

03

Access logging

Profile access is logged — verifyu.in describes time, IP address and location. Owner verification

04

The person is told

The registered user is notified in the app when their profile is accessed.

05

Role-based access

Who on your team may use VerifyU, and when, is defined in the hospital arrangement. Owner verification

06

Clear limits

Not a diagnostic tool, not a medical record, not a substitute for your own identification and consent procedures.

Getting started

From a demo to go-live.

Five steps, each with a decision point. Nothing is deployed before scope and roles are agreed.

  1. Step 01

    Demo

    What a match returns, what it does not, where the limits sit.

  2. Step 02

    Workflow review

    Your ED physicians, nursing leads and security map VerifyU onto the arrival workflow.

  3. Step 03

    Pilot scope

    Departments, roles, duration and measures — agreed in writing.

  4. Step 04

    Training

    Briefing for the staff who hold access: workflow, governance, limits. Owner verification

  5. Step 05

    Go-live

    A start date, a named contact on both sides, a review at the end.
