Privacy Policy

What VerifyU collects, why it is collected, and what you can ask for. This page summarises the published policy and links to it in full.

On this page SummaryFull policyBiometric dataConsistency review

Last updated: 13 September 2026 Owner verification

This is a summary, not a replacement. Nothing on this page changes the published policy. It has been written to be accurate to the published text, with unresolved points marked for the VerifyU team rather than smoothed over.

This page publishes a summary of the VerifyU privacy policy on the web so you can read it in a browser, on any device, without opening the VerifyU app or creating an account. The published policy at verifyu.in remains the authoritative document — where this summary and the published policy differ, the published policy applies.

Summary of the published policy

What is collected

  • Information you provide: your name, email address and phone number.
  • Facial data, collected for identity verification.
  • Usage, log, device and diagnostic information generated as you use the app.

Why it is collected

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To verify identity.
  • To provide support when you contact the team.
  • To improve the app.

Sharing

The published policy states that data is not shared with third-party service providers. See the consistency review below — this statement needs to be reconciled with how the product necessarily operates. Legal review

How long it is kept

Information is kept for as long as necessary for the purposes described in the policy, or for as long as required by law, and is then securely deleted or anonymised.

Security

The policy describes protective measures for the information held, and states plainly that no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. VerifyU makes no claim of perfect security, and this website makes none either.

Children

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over. Children under 13 are covered through a parent's or guardian's account rather than an account of their own.

Your rights

You can ask to access the information held about you, ask for it to be corrected, and ask for it to be deleted. Requests can be made through the settings in the app or by contacting the team. How to request deletion.

Contact

The published policy gives help@verifyu.in; this website and the app listings also use info@beaufortit.com. Both addresses reach the VerifyU team. Owner verification

Read the full published Privacy Policy

The complete policy lives on the official VerifyU site. Read it before relying on the summary above — it is the version that governs how your information is handled.

Read the full published Privacy Policy

Biometric and facial data notice

What is known today. VerifyU collects facial data during registration and uses it for identity verification and for matching a face to a registered profile when someone with scanning access runs a check. Face verification is completed by you, in the app, as part of registration.

What is not yet published. A formal biometric notice — covering the legal basis for processing facial data, how the facial template is stored and protected, how long it is retained, whether it is processed by any external service, and how consent is given and withdrawn — has not been drafted. Legal review

Facial data is sensitive personal data. In the context of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, a dedicated, clearly written biometric notice is likely to be expected of a product like this. This section is a placeholder and must be replaced with text drafted by a qualified adviser before launch. Legal review

Consistency review (for the VerifyU team)

These are differences found between the published privacy policy, the two app-store listings, press material and this website. They are listed here openly because they need resolving before launch, not because they are settled. Each one needs an owner decision.

  1. 1 · Google Play Data Safety says "No data collected". The app collects name, phone number, email, facial data and medical information. The Data Safety declaration should be rewritten to match what the app actually collects and why, including the sensitive categories. Legal reviewSources: Google Play listing vs the app's own registration flow and privacy policy.
  2. 2 · "No third-party sharing" versus how the product necessarily works. Sending an OTP requires an SMS provider; selling subscriptions through the stores means Apple and Google process payments; running the service requires cloud hosting (verifyu.in refers to AWS India). These are processors and should be disclosed as such, with a clear statement that they act on VerifyU's instructions. Legal reviewSources: published privacy policy vs verifyu.in and the store listings.
  3. 3 · App Store privacy declaration is incomplete. It lists Location and Contact Info, but not health or medical information and not biometric or facial data — both of which the app collects. Legal reviewSource: App Store listing, id6670164958.
  4. 4 · The developer name differs across sources. "BEAUFORT IT SOLUTIONS PVT LTD" on Google Play, "Beaufort IT Solutions LLP" on the App Store and in the ANI release, and "Beaufort IT Solutions Pvt. Ltd." on the website. One legal entity name should be used everywhere, and the incorrect listings corrected. Owner verificationSources: Google Play, App Store, ANI release, verifyu.in.
  5. 5 · Two support addresses are in circulation. help@verifyu.in appears in the store listings and the policy; info@beaufortit.com is used on this website. Either publish both with a stated purpose for each, or consolidate on one. Owner verificationSources: store listings and published policy vs this website.
  6. 6 · The published policy carries no effective or last-updated date. A dated version, with a short change history, makes the policy verifiable and is expected practice. Legal reviewSource: https://verifyu.in/privacy-policy/
  7. 7 · A biometric and facial data notice may be legally required. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, processing facial data warrants a specific notice and a clear consent record. This needs a qualified adviser, not a website draft. Legal reviewSee the biometric section above.
  8. 8 · App-store description language needs aligning. The App Store description still carries "deterrent against trafficking" style claims. The app descriptions should be brought in line with the language used on this website: VerifyU supports identification and communication, and does not promise prevention, rescue or a guaranteed outcome. Owner verificationSource: App Store listing.

This section is written for the VerifyU team and its advisers. It is published rather than hidden because a privacy page that quietly papers over known gaps is worse than one that names them.
