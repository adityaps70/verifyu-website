How the connection works

From a face to the people who matter.

  1. Step 01 · Face

    Start with a face.

    A VerifyU user with scanning access opens the scanner. No card, no phone needed from the person.

  2. Step 02 · Match

    Match found.

    The scan is checked against registered profiles. A registered person can be matched in moments.

  3. Step 03 · Identity

    A verified identity.

    Name, gender, blood group — exactly as the registered person set it up.

  4. Step 04 · Information

    Medical details, protected.

    Fuller medical information opens only with an OTP from an emergency contact.

  5. Step 05 · People

    The people they trust.

    Emergency contacts with their relationship and number, one tap from a call.

  6. Step 06 · Connection

    Scan. Identify. Connect.

    Someone who couldn’t speak for themselves is connected back to their people.
