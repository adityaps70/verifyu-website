VerifyU Care

Care beyond the app.

VerifyU Care is a separate, enquiry-based service line: tele-consultation, care at home, and equipment and care plans. Nothing is booked on this page. You send an enquiry, and we come back with what can be arranged for you — providers, availability and fees vary by location.

Enquire on WhatsApp See the services
Not an emergency service

VerifyU Care is not part of the emergency identification service.

Care enquiries are answered during working hours and are not monitored as an emergency channel. In a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services or go to the nearest hospital. The VerifyU app’s identification and SOS features are described on the Safety & Emergency page.

Services

Three things people ask us for.

Each one starts the same way — a WhatsApp message telling us who the care is for and where you are.

Tele-consultation

Speak to a doctor by phone or video, where that service is available in your area. Enquire and we will come back with what can be arranged and what it costs.

Care at home

Nursing visits, attendant support and recovery care delivered at home. Availability, timings and fees depend on the providers working in your city.

Equipment & care plans

Medical equipment and longer-term care arrangements for an ageing parent or someone recovering at home. Tell us the situation and we will share the options.

How it works

Send one message. We take it from there.

No account, no form and no payment on this page. An enquiry is a conversation.

  1. Send your enquiryTap one of the buttons above. The message opens ready to send on WhatsApp.
  2. Tell us the situationWho the care is for, which city, and what you think is needed.
  3. We come back with optionsWhat can be arranged near you, what it involves and what it costs. Owner verification

What to include

The more of this you send in the first message, the faster we can answer usefully.

  • City or area — availability differs by location
  • Who the care is for — you, a parent, a dependant
  • What is needed — a consultation, a home visit, equipment, an ongoing plan
  • Preferred timing — how soon, and which days or hours suit you
